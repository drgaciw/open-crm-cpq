## Decisions for this lane

1. **QuoteAccepted stays v1.** Add optional typed `ramp` to the existing line snapshot and optional `pricing.exitArr`. The outdated D8 sentence calling this a version bump conflicts with ADR-023's explicit prohibition and the accepted sprint; the additive v1 decision governs. Shared DTOs contain data only. CRM Deal columns and its metric copying remain unchanged.
2. **Rate identity is line-scoped.** NEW acceptance creates one subscription per quote; AMENDMENT, EXPANSION and RENEWAL update their explicit existing target. Unique rate and apply identity is `(subscription_id, accepted_line_uuid, sequence)`, rather than `(subscription_id, sequence)`, because two sold lines can both have sequence 1. Each RampApplied identifies subscription, accepted line and sequence. This clarifies D8/D6 without creating one subscription per line.
3. **Legacy periods preserve aggregate facts.** Backfill one implicit aggregate period per historical subscription, using a reserved legacy identity and its existing dates, MRR, ARR and currency. Represent provenance explicitly; quantity and unit price stay absent. Do not infer a seat count or price, overwrite subscriptions, or emit retroactive RampApplied events. Mark the implicit period already applied. For new flat recurring lines with enough event facts, store line-level implicit periods; a pre-lineItems payload retains the aggregate fallback.
4. **Renewal of aggregate legacy data is aggregate-only.** GetRampSchedule exposes known aggregate exit MRR/ARR and provenance. It cannot supply a seat unit rate. An operation requesting a unit-rate renewal/amendment from such a row fails with an actionable missing-line-rate error rather than inventing a unit price. Existing date-only subscription renewal remains an extension, at the known exit aggregate rate.
5. **Dates use inclusive end and original anchor.** Period start is `termStart.plusMonths(startMonth - 1)`; period end is the next anchored boundary minus one day. Term end is `termStart.plusMonths(termMonths).minusDays(1)`. New ramp events require coherent dates/term and full priced coverage. Invalid new ramp payloads are non-retryable validation failures, so inbox and partial subscription writes roll back. Captured legacy payloads with absent dates keep an explicit compatibility fallback using the injected clock and old 12-month convention; distinguish that fallback from validated ramp dates.
6. **Apply only commercially active subscriptions.** PENDING and SUSPENDED retain unapplied steps and catch up in date order after activation/resumption; CANCELLED and EXPIRED do not apply. The lag gauge covers due unapplied steps on ACTIVE subscriptions, so suspended/pending rows do not create false overdue alerts. At activation, due sequence 1 follows the same apply/outbox transaction as later steps. Subscription creation initializes contracted start metrics but does not claim scheduler application. Historical aggregate backfills are already applied.
7. **Amendment and renewal remain quote-service orchestration.** Pricing-service is exclusively R4-owned. R3 adds a contract-asset schedule read client and allocated `quote-service/.../ramp/` orchestration to author quote inputs from remaining/exit rates. Shared libraries do not calculate money. Whole-month amendment boundaries use the remaining month counts; inside-period amendments use actual days in that anchored billing month at its current rate. Renewal authors a flat rate of exit unit rate with uplift; extension holds exit rate; price protection is unchanged.

8. **Existing subscription identity is explicit and persisted.** Add optional `targetSubscriptionNumber` to quote creation/update DTOs and quote persistence, and carry it in an additive accepted-event subscription-change snapshot. Use the public stable subscription number, never a foreign database ID, customer-only lookup, CRM deal ID, or parsing arbitrary metadata. Quote amendment/renewal orchestration reads the target via contract-asset API/service trust and checks customer/currency before authoring. Consumer independently fetches and locks that target in its own schema. Non-NEW events missing, unknown, deleted or wrong-customer targets fail as non-retryable invalid events; they never create a fallback subscription. NEW with a target is rejected as conflicting input. A historical payload with no deal type keeps the old NEW compatibility branch.
9. **Mutation semantics preserve lineage and history.** EXPANSION adds accepted new recurring lines and their rates to the target, co-termed to its existing end. AMENDMENT replaces only explicitly mapped target lines from its effective date; each accepted affected line carries an optional `sourceSubscriptionLineUuid` propagated from persisted quote-line authoring. Unaffected line rates remain; earlier historical periods remain auditable. Replacing a line retires/splits its future effective coverage before adding the replacement so active aggregates never count both. AMENDMENT without a source-line mapping, currency change or out-of-term effective date fails instead of guessing. A quantity addition such as AC-13 uses expansion/additional-line semantics. RENEWAL appends the accepted next-term rates to the same subscription, updates end/renewal dates, and keeps old rates as history; its new term starts on the prior inclusive end plus one day. Only an ACTIVE target renews; amendments/expansions target ACTIVE or SUSPENDED, preserving status. CANCELLED/EXPIRED targets reject all mutations.
10. **Every accepted mutation has durable natural deduplication.** Add an applied-acceptance ledger unique on accepted quote UUID, linked to target subscription and operation, in the same transaction as inbox claim, rate changes, dates/metrics and ERP QUEUED update. `existsBySubscriptionNumber(SUB-quoteId)` only deduplicates NEW and is insufficient for mutations or replay without an eventId. Redelivery with a different/missing header cannot add quantity, split history or extend twice. Serialize concurrent changes by locking the target; reject conflicting stale lineage rather than overwriting it. Keep external ERP calls outside this transaction and queue changed existing subscriptions for the established follow-up job.
11. **Commercial labels/ARR use accepted facts.** For new events, derive plan display deterministically from accepted recurring product names in quote order (distinct names joined; no hardcoded Standard Plan for populated lines). Missing lineItems uses an explicitly documented legacy-only label fallback. New/subsequent term ARR uses `pricing.arr`, not `totalAmount`/TCV; a new ramp without pricing ARR is invalid. For expansion/amendment delta quotes, event ARR describes the sold delta/replacement, so recompute subscription aggregate ARR from effective line rates after the mutation rather than overwrite the whole subscription with that delta. Retain the old total-amount fallback only for captured historical payloads without pricing; tag that provenance as legacy, never apply it to a newly typed mutation.
12. **Cancellation is an explicit domain transition.** Permit cancellation only from PENDING, ACTIVE or SUSPENDED. CANCELLED and EXPIRED reject without changing state or persisting another transition. Add domain/service/controller regression coverage for all five states; subsequent scheduler runs never apply rates to a cancelled subscription. Cancellation event contract/registry/publication stays a separate follow-up outside R3.

The call leaves the subscription unchanged. `Subscription.renew` and `SubscriptionService.renew` were removed. The contract V1_6 migration can include rate and acceptance-ledger schema together. ### Credited-period validation and replay contract

Reserve optional typed `grossPeriodAmount` and `migrationCredit` in quote `PricedPeriodView`, persisted snapshot and accepted ramp period. Ordinary periods omit both; their identity remains `periodAmount = netMonthly * months`. For an authorized credited period, `grossPeriodAmount = netMonthly * months`, `periodAmount = grossPeriodAmount - migrationCredit`, and `0 <= migrationCredit <= grossPeriodAmount`. Credit changes TCV/ACV only; rate storage/apply uses unchanged `netUnitPrice`/`netMonthly`, and recurring ARR/exit ARR never subtract the one-time credit. No validator globally enforces the ordinary amount identity on credited periods.

Authorization needs typed facts as well as money. Root forwards the persisted validated line `hybridMigration` context into the additive accepted line snapshot (pair id, DECLINING/RISING role, window and book-value facts using the same names as the R4 quote handoff). The consumer validates that credit is on the first DECLINING period of a validated pair/window, rejects partial gross/credit fields, negative/excess credit and any RISING/later-period credit. Producer and consumer service-owned validators bind the amount to typed book value: requested credit is `round2(bookValue * 0.30)` with HALF_UP arithmetic, and effective credit is `min(requestedCredit, grossDecliningLineTCV, firstGrossPeriodAmount)`. They validate these accepted priced facts and gross-minus-credit amounts; they do not reprice authored rates. The credited period need not be wholly inside the overlap window or start with that window: window bounds govern entitlement overlap and the step-down exception; credit location remains the declining line's first period. It does not infer authorization from free-form waterfall/explanation text. Root must pin this context shape with the shared fixture before consumer edits; gross/credit fields alone cannot establish the required role. These are data records only; validators remain service-owned.

Root approved this typed-context boundary and the narrowly scoped ERP completion regression during readiness. Full contract-asset ownership is assigned to `r3_next_readiness` once the gates open; root retains common, accepted producer, quote client and continuation orchestration. That assignment does not open the product-edit gate.

The approval fingerprint includes persisted target subscription number, source-line identity, observed revision, typed migration inputs and full accepted-priced-period facts, including gross/credit. Updating any of these invalidates stale pricing/approval through the established quote governance path. Replay ledger uniqueness is accepted quote UUID independently of event header id. Store a deterministic commercial-payload digest with the ledger: identical replay is a no-op, but reusing the quote UUID with different commercial facts is a validation error rather than silently accepting a different mutation. Exclude delivery timestamp/header identity from that digest.
