## Recorded implementation decisions

R3 uses (subscription_id, accepted_line_uuid, sequence) identity so multiple lines can each have period 1. Legacy backfill keeps an explicitly aggregate rate with known MRR/ARR and absent quantity/unit facts; it invents no unit price. Legacy unit-rate renewal fails with an explicit actionable error until a commercial rate is supplied. ApplyRamp runs only for ACTIVE subscriptions; suspended/pending subscriptions retain unapplied periods for catch-up on activation, while cancelled/expired subscriptions do not advance. Accepted event stays version 1 with additive optional fields; existing consumers/tolerance tests must pass.

R4 effective credit is min(round2(bookValue * 0.30), decliningLineTCV, decliningFirstPeriodAmount); unused credit is not carried or transferred. Credit is a one-time amount adjustment: subtract it from first period amount, TCV and term-years ACV while preserving recurring unit/monthly rates and ARR/exit ARR. This is an explicit narrow migration-credit exception to ADR-023 D-02's ordinary periodAmount = netMonthly * months identity. Gross amount, effective/unused credit and adjusted amount must remain auditable and survive the persisted accepted snapshot; no runtime code may assume adjusted amount always equals recurring rate times months.

R4 customer-specific pair/window/book-value inputs use typed validated request metadata; authoritative pricing product data validates roles/types. Root's additional quote handoff must persist and forward that input and include it in approval fingerprint/staleness. Quote ownership is scheduled serially against R3, not edited concurrently. R4's exclusive allowlist is extended narrowly to PeriodGovernance.evaluate and associated tests; MetricsCalculator should remain unchanged unless a new impact-reviewed need appears.

These clarifications are owner-delegated implementation choices recorded before coding, not reports of finance or sales-ops approval. ### Allocated continuation boundary

Root delegates sole quote-service implementation to the quote agent's separate worktree, while retaining common contracts and integration/review. Whole-month ramps cannot encode a partial first billing month. To satisfy actual-day amendments without a zero-month RampStep, allocate additive acceptedLine.initialProration {startDate,endDate,billingPeriodStart,billingPeriodEnd,quantity,netUnitPrice,netMonthly,periodAmount}, and subscriptionChange.effectiveDate. The initial interval starts at the mutation effective date and ends at the next original-anchor billing boundary minus one day; amount is round2(netMonthly * inclusiveRemainingDays / inclusiveBillingPeriodDays), HALF_UP. Following whole-month terms begin at that next boundary and end at the existing inclusive term end. Consumer stores the explicit initial interval as sequence0; ordinary ramp sequences remain1+. Producer/consumer validate no overlap, exact original-anchor boundaries, trusted rates, customer/currency/revision and the actual-day amount. This is a data-only common contract; service owners implement validation and authoring. No subscription UI or feature activation is allocated.

If only the final partial billing interval remains, use an explicit target-linked initialProration-only line with no ordinary ramp, terms from effective date through target end and nominal termMonths=1. Do not relax the global positive-duration constraint or fabricate a zero-month step. The verified continuation branch uses TCV equal to the partial amount, term-years ACV equal to TCV*12 for that one nominal billing interval, and ARR/exit ARR equal to netMonthly*12. This is a scoped billing-term convention, not actual-day annualization. All feature, identity, revision and authorization gates still apply; both owners validate original-anchor dates, including Jan31 and leap-Feb boundaries.

For an initial partial followed by full periods, retain the allocated accepted terms starting at the next boundary and termMonths equal to the full-period count. TCV includes the explicit partial charge; TERM_YEARS ACV remains TCV*12/accepted termMonths. Do not introduce an unrepresented extra-month denominator. Initial scalar monthly amount and ARR come from the earlier partial rate, while exit ARR comes from the final full period. A real positive one-month pricing request evaluates current policy for the partial rate and quantity; its unit price must match the trusted source rate. Merge the strongest partial/full governance into the new approval binding, then calculate the actual-day amount separately. That policy-only month is never persisted as a fictitious ordinary ramp. A changed current price/policy that cannot preserve the inherited rate fails actionably.

Fixed recurring classification preserves actual lineItemType strings.
