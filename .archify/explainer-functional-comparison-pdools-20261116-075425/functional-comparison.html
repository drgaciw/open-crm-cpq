Answer

open-crm-cpq (this workspace) is a multi-service CRM/CPQ platform. price-dools-service (/home/username01/MyCode/price-dools-service, artifact price-rules-ai-drools) is a single Spring Boot 3.4 / Drools 8.44 microservice focused on rule deploy/execute, ARR·TCV·ACV·CLV·churn, templates/conflicts/tests, and AI rule create/improve with Sequential Thinking + Context7 + semantic cache. It does not replace CPQ's quotes, CRM, gateway, or Angular UI.

  1. CPQ wins product surface: quotes, CRM, price books, approvals, gateway, Angular, waterfall pricing, flagged AI workflows/experiments.
  2. price-dools wins niche rule-ops tooling: conflict analysis, template factories, rule-test suites, metrics history/snapshots, semantic routing cache, Context7 doc enhancement.
  3. Overlap: Drools execute/validate, financial ARR/TCV/ACV, AI create/improve rules — different APIs and maturity; do not dual-run both as system of record.
  4. Harvest selectively into pricing-service / ai-service; do not merge the monolith wholesale.
